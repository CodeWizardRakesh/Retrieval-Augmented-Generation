Karunya Institute of Technology and Sciences (KITS) is a NAAC A++ accredited institution in India, recognized for its focus on solving global challenges in food, water, healthcare, and energy through education and research. The university offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and research programs across engineering, management, agriculture, sciences, humanities, media, and health sciences.

Institutional Overview
Mission: To develop scientists and entrepreneurs capable of creating solutions for humanity’s critical needs in food security, clean water access, healthcare innovation, and sustainable energy.
Leadership: Headed by Chancellor Dr. Paul Dhinakaran (M.B.A., Ph.D.), the institution emphasizes socially responsible education and research.

Academic Structure
Programs: Engineering, Arts, Science, Media, Management, and Health Sciences.

Pedagogy: Student-centered learning with modern infrastructure and industry-aligned curricula.
